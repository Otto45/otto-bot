Add customer orders. An order has an id, the customer's id, line items (product name, quantity, unit price in cents) and a total in cents. (1) A new OrderPricingProcessor computes an order's total, with a 10% discount on orders over 100 dollars. (2) A new ExchangeRatesGateway gets exchange rates from https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/USD (its rates field maps currency codes to rates), so a total can be shown in another currency. (3) A new OrderPlacementEngine places an order: it looks the customer up, prices the order, saves it in the relational database (a new orders table, with a migration and a new query in the relational database gateway) and records an audit event. (4) The manager and the facade get place-order (a customer's id and line items) and get-order-total (an order's id and a currency) operations, named in the stack's own conventions. (5) The HTTP service gets POST /customers/{id}/orders and GET /orders/{id}/total?currency=EUR. Keep to the architecture rules and make sure the checks pass.
